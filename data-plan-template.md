> Which sensor will your finished device use? Train with that sensor —
> a different one gives the model different numbers.

## Classes

> What must the device tell apart? Include **"nothing happening"** — the
> device spends most of its time there.

## Collection

> Who records, how many examples per class, and in which conditions
> (people, places, speeds, how the sensor is held)?
> Which recordings will you keep aside for testing?

## Risk
